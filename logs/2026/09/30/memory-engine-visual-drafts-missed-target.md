David clarified that **none** of the generated Memory Engine images hit the
intended visual direction; in his judgment, each successive image moved
further away. Earlier positive-sounding remarks about a name or visual theme
must not be read as approval of any of these images. Retain the drafts as
historical experiments, not target references or brand assets.

to add more literal brass machinery, cyborg anatomy, genre scenery, and image
detail. That may have crowded out the quality David was trying to describe.

so this diagnosis should not become a preference claim.

concrete examples, rather than editing one of these drafts again. The explicit
thematic directions remain: pulp science fiction, human-machine ambiguity,
