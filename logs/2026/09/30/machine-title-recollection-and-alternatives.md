# Rechecking the early machine title

David asked whether the original title idea had been “The Machine,” “The
Thinking Machine,” or “The Mind Machine” (his spoken wording may also have
been “mine machine”). These are recollections or possibilities, not selected
names.

A search of the current Warehouse Markdown found no earlier record of any of
those phrases as a title. The earliest recorded direction was a “cyborg brain”
metaphor during [Warehouse's rebrand exploration](./warehouse-public-identity-name-exploration.md). “Memory Engine” is the
first concrete candidate recorded in this exploration, suggested by David
later that day in [the naming and visual log](./memory-engine-name-and-pulp-visual-exploration.md). *The Machine Stops* was
introduced later as a thematic literary reference, not a proposed title; see
[the human-machine theme](./human-machine-theme-for-memory-engine.md).

This is a finding about the current Warehouse record, not a claim about every
earlier conversation or private thought.
