# The person and machine grow together

David confirmed that “grown together” is a major theme for the possible
Memory Engine identity: evolving or merging over time. He connected this to
his reading of *The Timeless Way of Building*: things become what they are as
they grow, and an architect's job is to construct them so they can keep
growing as they expand. This records David's interpretation of the book, not
an independent summary of its contents.

For the visual exploration, this adds a temporal quality to the earlier
[human-machine merger theme](./human-machine-theme-for-memory-engine.md).
The agent's tentative interpretation is a relationship that has developed
through use, adaptation, and mutual change, rather than a single moment of
assembly. The specific visual form remains open; David has approved no
generated image as a reference.
