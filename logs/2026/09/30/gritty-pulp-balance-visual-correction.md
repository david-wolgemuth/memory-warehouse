# Memory Engine pixel art needs grit and pulp wonder together

David said the gritty cyborg draft (unavailable in this public edition)
overcorrected. There is a fine line between gritty and depressing, and that
draft lost the pulp quality he wanted. The intended direction keeps the
pixel-art preference and the ambiguous human-machine merger while restoring
energy, invention, adventure, and wonder. This correction is scoped to the
Memory Engine identity exploration; no final image has been approved.

## Revised exploratory image

Gritty pulp pixel-art concept (unavailable in this public edition)
is a 1024 × 1536 original raster image made with the built-in image generator
and copied into this dated log. The revision prompt used the earlier cyborg
draft as a reference. It asked for an alert operator integrated with the
engine at the controls of a battered rocket-airship or observatory, a star
map, a strange world beyond a round window, flying index cards, pressure
gauges, worn brass and circuitry, a warmer focal glow, and a limited
navy/turquoise/amber/vermilion pixel-art palette. It explicitly rejected a
bleak dystopian portrait, horror, and a grim prison-like setting. The title
band is blank because the name remains tentative. This is a concept, not an
approved publication asset.
