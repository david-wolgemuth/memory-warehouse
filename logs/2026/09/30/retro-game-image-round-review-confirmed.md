# David agreed with the retro-game image round assessment

After seeing the four [retro-game Memory Engine candidates](./retro-game-memory-engine-image-round.md), David agreed with the agent's
review: the side-view image has the strongest focal point; the modular
diorama best explores simple-component structure; the vignette and emblem
fall back toward a person riding a separate machine; and the group remains
finer and busier than the small sprite references. This agreement validates
that comparative assessment for this round, not any candidate as an approved
identity image or a universal rule for future imagery.
