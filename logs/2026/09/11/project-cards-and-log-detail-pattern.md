# Project-card and log-detail pattern

## Note

- User requested durable cards for confirmed projects discovered through the
  Claude export, with GitHub URL, local location, high-level description, and
  pointers into `ai-exports` where available.
- Cards should remain lean, current, and factual. Supporting detail,
  uncertainty, import analysis, and newly discovered evidence belong in dated
  logs; cards are revised only when their compact project facts improve.
- Four projects have direct local-repository evidence and are promoted now:
  Patient World, Workshed, Frederick Matrix, and Genesis. Imported
  employer-domain project records remain logged rather than promoted until a
  local or primary repository source establishes their identity and location.
