# Log topic drift and tangents should start new notes

## User direction

David observed that agents appear to append to an existing log too often. He
wants a new, small, subject-named log whenever the topic has drifted, including
for tangents, rather than extending the earlier log by default.

## Scope

This refines the existing direction that a changed subject or substantial
later development should begin a new log. It applies to log-writing behavior;
it does not require splitting a coherent single-topic research study merely
because it is long. A later, distinct conclusion, correction, implementation,
or tangent should preserve its own context in a new dated note and link back
when useful.

## Current repository observation

Recent logs are generally separated by subject and date. Several long notes
from 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-17 are bounded source/design studies rather than
indiscriminate session journals. The risk David identifies is therefore mainly
future continuation after a subject shift, not a need to rewrite those
historical logs.
