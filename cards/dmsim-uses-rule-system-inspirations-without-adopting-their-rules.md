# DMSim uses rule-system inspirations without adopting their rules

DMSim’s design history selectively examines tabletop systems for setup and
prompting patterns. Those comparisons do not establish implementation of those
systems’ mechanics; in particular, its group-history steps are an original
adaptation of shared-backstory procedures, not a Fate or BOLD implementation.
