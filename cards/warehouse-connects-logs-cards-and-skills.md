# Warehouse connects evidence, reusable claims, and procedures

Warehouse keeps dated logs for observations and evidence, cards for concise
reusable claims, skills for repeatable procedures, and indexes for navigation.
Markdown is the source of truth. A separate public projection shares selected,
reviewed records from the private source.
