# Voice dictation can introduce transcription ambiguity

David frequently uses voice-to-text. Messages can contain missing or misplaced
punctuation, homophones or word substitutions, run-together phrases, and other
transcription errors.

## Why it matters

Agents should prioritize the likely intended meaning over literal malformed
wording. When context makes the meaning reasonably clear, proceed without
making David restate it. When a material detail remains genuinely ambiguous,
state the interpretation briefly and ask one focused question rather than
inventing certainty. David may notice and send corrections.

## Boundaries

This is a communication aid, not permission to silently alter a material
instruction, safety-sensitive detail, commitment, or preference. Preserve the
user's apparent intent and make consequential uncertainty explicit.
