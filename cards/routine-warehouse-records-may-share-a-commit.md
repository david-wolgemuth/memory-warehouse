# Daily maintenance batches ordinary Warehouse changes

Daily maintenance batches eligible ordinary Warehouse cards, logs, and
operating guidance in a reviewed checkpoint. A dirty worktree is not a reason
to postpone recording a direction or creating a useful artifact, nor should
routine committing delay an answer.

## Boundaries

Review the staged diff, but exclude only files that are clearly secret,
generated, temporary, or intentionally ignored. Do not revert unknown changes;
if they are ordinary Warehouse records or guidance, include them in the
checkpoint rather than leaving them behind.
